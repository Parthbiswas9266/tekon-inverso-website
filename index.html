01 / ABOUT THE STUDIO

Turning complex
problems into workable
engineering.

Tekon Inverso Advanced Research & Technologies is an independent research and product-development enterprise focused on applied engineering and specialised hardware.

We bring together research, electronics, embedded development and prototype engineering to investigate practical problems and develop purpose-built solutions. Our work is guided by curiosity, technical rigour and a bias toward testing ideas in the real world.
